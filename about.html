About This Site

// why this exists

This site was built to help candidates prepare for ICT Officer (Programmer) job interviews in Tanzania. It offers free, structured practice questions covering the topics commonly tested in these interviews — from JavaScript fundamentals to practical coding problems.

// Who it's for

Anyone applying for ICT Officer, Programmer, or related tech roles in Tanzania's public and private sector — students, recent graduates, and professionals brushing up before an interview.

// What you'll find here

  • Multiple-choice quiz questions organized by difficulty level
  • Practical coding questions where you predict code output
  • Explanations after each question to help you understand the "why," not just the answer
  • Bilingual support (Swahili and English)

// Our goal

To make interview preparation accessible and free, so that language or cost is never a barrier to landing a good tech job in Tanzania.
